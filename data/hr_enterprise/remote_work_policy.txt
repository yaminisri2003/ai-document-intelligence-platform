REMOTE WORK POLICY
Company: TechCorp Solutions
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Version: 2.0

1. ELIGIBILITY
All full-time employees who have completed 90 days of employment 
are eligible to apply for remote work arrangements. Part-time 
employees may be eligible at manager discretion. Contractors 
and interns are not eligible for remote work arrangements.

2. EQUIPMENT AND TECHNOLOGY
The company will provide a laptop and necessary software licenses 
for all approved remote workers. Employees are responsible for 
maintaining a reliable internet connection with minimum speeds 
of 25 Mbps download and 10 Mbps upload. A dedicated workspace 
free from distractions is strongly recommended.

3. WORK HOURS AND AVAILABILITY
Remote employees must be available during core hours of 10am 
to 3pm in their local timezone Monday through Friday. Attendance 
at all scheduled team meetings is mandatory regardless of location. 
Employees must respond to messages within 2 hours during core hours.

4. PERFORMANCE AND PRODUCTIVITY
Remote employees are held to the same performance standards as 
in-office employees. Managers will conduct quarterly performance 
reviews with specific attention to productivity metrics and 
communication effectiveness. Failure to meet standards may 
result in remote work privileges being revoked.

5. SECURITY AND CONFIDENTIALITY
All remote work must be conducted on company-approved devices only. 
Use of public WiFi is strictly prohibited without VPN connection. 
Employees must lock their screens when stepping away. Company data 
must never be stored on personal devices or unauthorized cloud 
storage services.

6. EXPENSES AND REIMBURSEMENT
The company will reimburse up to 50 dollars per month for internet 
expenses with valid receipts. Home office equipment purchases above 
100 dollars require prior manager approval. Reimbursement requests 
must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.

7. HEALTH AND WELLBEING
Remote employees are encouraged to take regular breaks and maintain 
a healthy work-life balance. The company provides access to mental 
health resources through the Employee Assistance Program. Remote 
workers should have a dedicated workspace that meets basic ergonomic 
standards.

8. POLICY VIOLATIONS
Violations of this remote work policy may result in disciplinary 
action up to and including termination of remote work privileges 
or employment. Employees are expected to report any security 
incidents immediately to the IT department.
